How Much Does a Website Cost in India in 2026? Real Price Breakdown

"How much for a website?" is the most common question Indian agencies hear — and the answers range from Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 5,00,000. Both can be legitimate. What matters is knowing what you are paying for, so here is an honest breakdown of 2026 prices.

Freelancer / template sites: Rs. 5,000–25,000

A freelancer installing WordPress with a premium theme. Fine for a simple brochure site, but expect limited customization, no real SEO setup, and support that disappears after delivery.

Small agency: Rs. 25,000–80,000

The sweet spot for most small businesses. You get a custom design, proper mobile responsiveness, basic SEO, contact forms with WhatsApp integration, and a few rounds of revisions. This is where most serious local businesses land.

E-commerce / custom web apps: Rs. 80,000–5,00,000+

Online stores, booking systems, dashboards — anything with logins, payments, or custom logic. Price depends on features, not pages. Always ask for a fixed scope in writing.

Hidden costs nobody mentions upfront

Domain + hosting (Rs. 3,000–8,000/year), SSL (usually free now), premium plugins, content writing, product photography, and annual maintenance (budget 15–20% of build cost per year). Get these in the quote before you sign.

How to not get burned

Get 3 quotes, check 3 live sites they built (on your phone, not just desktop), confirm who owns the domain and hosting accounts (it must be YOU), and never pay 100% upfront — 40/40/20 milestones are standard.
